# Changelog

New, changed, fixed or removed features, one line each, brief and direct. The section for a release becomes the release notes.

## 1.1.0

- Changed: the stratagem icon has soft white bullets and a white plus on the standard yellow background.
- New: the stratagem icon is the picture Obsidian shows for the mod.

## 1.0.0

- New: shared Resupply mission stratagem, carried by every player.
- New: Resupply drops four ammunition boxes.
- New: 180 second cooldown shared by the squad, kept by players who join mid-mission.
- New: standard Resupply removed from the Arsenal and loadouts, with the Preserve vanilla Resupply option to keep it.
